# Capability Backlog and Scope Status

This document distinguishes Phase 2 commitments from capabilities that remain outside the approved scope.

## Scheduled for Phase 2

- Amazon Bedrock usage and AWS CUR-style financial adaptation.
- Vertex AI/Gemini usage and GCP detailed-billing adaptation.
- Limited supporting AWS and GCP infrastructure cost.
- One small hosted-inference/GPU endpoint with utilization and break-even economics.
- Budget decompositions of the same approved annual total of **$76,438.52**.
- Driver-based forecasting, scenarios, forecast accuracy, and explainable variance.
- Fully loaded AI workload cost.
- Quality, latency, reliability, anomaly, and simulated guardrail analysis.
- Provider and contract economics.
- Expanded Power BI, Excel, and PowerPoint decision deliverables.

## Deferred Outside Phase 2

- Azure OpenAI; Azure capability belongs to a separate portfolio project.
- Large-scale model training or fine-tuning economics.
- A large GPU fleet or advanced cluster scheduling.
- Multimodal video economics.
- Multiple currencies and foreign-exchange conversion.
- Autonomous provider-side shutdown or enforcement.
- An AI FinOps chatbot or autonomous management agent.
- Actual revenue, productivity, labor-displacement, customer-satisfaction, ROI, or realized business-value claims.
- Production customer data, confidential invoices, or proprietary contracts.

## Cross-Project Integration Boundary

Project 2 may export a summarized monthly dataset containing application, department, provider channel, actual cost, budget, forecast, identified opportunity, and verified modeled savings. Project 1 may consume that summary for executive reporting.

Project 1 must not recreate token pricing, retry logic, experiment governance, quality evaluation, or AI-provider reconciliation. Project 2 must not recreate a complete infrastructure FinOps platform.
